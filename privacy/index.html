Privacy

A lightweight site with lightweight data collection.

The planning calculators run in your browser and do not require an account.

Effective: September 14, 2026 · Last reviewed: September 14, 2026

Information entered into tools

Planner inputs such as dimensions, budget, state and preferences are processed in the browser to generate results. The current site does not ask you to create an account or submit those inputs to Tubs & Saunas.

Technical data

The hosting provider may process routine server logs such as IP address, browser type, requested page, timestamps and security events to deliver and protect the site.

External links

If you follow a link to a retailer or another website, that site's privacy policy governs its collection and use of information. Tubs & Saunas does not control external checkout or account systems.

Cookies and analytics

The current site does not set advertising cookies or operate its own user account system. If analytics or additional services are added, this policy should be updated before those tools are enabled.

Contact

Privacy questions may be sent to privacy@tubsandsaunas.com.

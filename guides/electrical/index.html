Electrical field note

120V vs 240V: what actually changes?

Voltage is not a quality score. It changes available power, circuit requirements and the shape of the installation project.

Safety: Follow the product manual, local electrical code and a qualified electrician's instructions. Wet-location equipment adds additional requirements.

120V equipment

Some compact infrared saunas and many portable chillers can operate on 120V. That can reduce installation complexity, but the manufacturer may still require a dedicated circuit and specific amperage.

240V equipment

Traditional electric sauna heaters and many full-size hot tubs commonly use 240V because the equipment needs more power. That usually means a dedicated branch circuit and professional installation.

Do not plan from voltage alone

Check voltage, amperage, breaker size, conductor requirements, plug/receptacle or hardwire instructions, GFCI requirements, clearances and whether the circuit must be dedicated. The product's installation manual controls.

Why this belongs before purchase

A lower-priced unit can become the more expensive project if electrical service upgrades are required. Conversely, a compatible 120V unit can be attractive where electrical changes are difficult.

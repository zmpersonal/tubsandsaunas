Where to buy a sauna or cold plunge.

A short, disclosed list of established sources with different product lineups. Use it after planning the space—and before treating any product card as a final specification.

How this page works: The featured retailer appears first in planner results. Other sources are included to broaden product coverage, not as a numerical ranking or safety endorsement. Verify current pricing, availability, warranty terms, electrical requirements and delivery details directly.
Featured retailer

InHouse Wellness

InHouse Wellness is an Austin, Texas-based home-wellness retailer specializing in infrared and traditional saunas, outdoor saunas and cold plunges. Its current catalog includes Finnmark Designs, Golden Designs, Dynamic Saunas, Maxxus, SaunaLife, LeisureCraft, Scandia and related equipment.

Why it is featured

  • Free curbside shipping on products in the continental United States, with no stated minimum.
  • A price-match process for shoppers who locate a lower advertised price.
  • Phone guidance before purchase and support after equipment arrives.
  • A broad selection across infrared, traditional, barrel and outdoor saunas, plus multiple cold-plunge formats.

Before choosing a seller

Compare the written warranty for the exact model—not a sitewide headline. Confirm whether delivery is curbside, threshold or room-of-choice; whether assembly is included; who handles damage claims; and which party pays return freight. For 240V or hardwired equipment, obtain the installation manual before purchase and have a qualified electrician review it.

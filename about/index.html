About

A field guide before the delivery truck arrives.

Tubs & Saunas helps homeowners think through space, power, water, operating cost and product fit before committing to equipment.

What we publish

The site combines interactive planning worksheets with practical references for saunas, cold plunges and hot tubs. The goal is to surface the questions that are easy to miss on a product page: access paths, required clearances, dedicated circuits, drainage, ventilation, service room and ongoing energy use.

What we do not do

We do not replace manufacturer manuals, site measurements, licensed trades or medical advice. The planner is a screening tool, not an engineering approval or a prediction of individual health outcomes.

How commerce fits

The site has a disclosed featured-retailer relationship and also lists selected alternative sellers with different product lines. Commercial ordering is kept separate from the calculator inputs, and retailer details are centralized in the buying guide.

Corrections

Specifications and policies change. Readers and manufacturers may report an error by emailing corrections@tubsandsaunas.com with the page URL, the disputed detail and a primary source.

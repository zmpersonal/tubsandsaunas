The order of operations

Three decisions to make before falling in love with a model.

The equipment is the visible part of the project. Fit, infrastructure and ownership cost are what determine whether it remains a good purchase.

I.

Fit before features

Measure doors, paths, service access, clearances and usable circulation—not just the nominal footprint of the cabinet.

II.

Power before purchase

120V and 240V can imply very different installation work. Check amperage, breaker requirements and whether the circuit must be dedicated.

III.

Use before sticker price

Short high-draw heat, continuous water heating and periodic chilling behave differently on the utility bill. Model how you will actually use it.

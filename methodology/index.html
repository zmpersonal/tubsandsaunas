Transparency

How the field guide works.

Rough screening estimates stay separate from manufacturer-specific installation requirements and commercial recommendations.

Last reviewed: September 14, 2026 · Scope: planning calculations, product matching and retailer inclusion

Commercial relationship

InHouse Wellness is the featured retail partner on TubsAndSaunas.com. When the catalog contains a relevant product from that retailer, it is listed before products from other sources. The relationship is disclosed wherever product ordering is explained.

Product prominence does not change the mathematical inputs used by the footprint or electricity calculators.

Product data

The repository combines a scheduled public-catalog refresh for the featured retailer with a smaller, manually reviewed list of product lines from Sun Home Saunas, Redwood Outdoors, Renu Therapy and Clearlight. Those alternatives primarily use their own brand names and were selected to broaden the market view without duplicating the featured catalog's main brands.

Product cards route through the buying guide so readers can review the source, selection basis and disclosure before leaving this site. Prices, availability and specifications can change; users should verify every material detail with the retailer and manufacturer before purchase.

Recommendation order

The planner first screens category, stated budget and indoor/outdoor setting. It then shows up to two matching featured-retailer products, followed by up to two qualifying alternatives. The planner does not score build quality, medical benefit, safety or long-term reliability.

Electricity estimates

The basic calculator multiplies rated kilowatts by estimated run time, frequency and an electricity planning rate. It does not model thermostat cycling, chiller duty cycle, ambient weather, insulation, pump behavior or utility rate structures.

State planning rates are stored in a local data file. The included updater can replace them with U.S. Energy Information Administration residential retail-price data when the repository owner supplies a free EIA API key. If no key is configured, the site continues to work using the local planning values.

Footprint estimates

Footprints are broad screening ranges based on equipment category. Actual units require manufacturer-specified dimensions, clearances, ventilation, drainage, service access and code compliance.

No engineering or medical advice

The site does not determine whether an electrical installation, slab, deck, plumbing arrangement or drainage solution is safe. It also does not make individualized medical recommendations. Use qualified professionals and manufacturer documentation where appropriate.
